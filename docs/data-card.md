# Data card

AURORA uses two kinds of data and never mixes them up on screen.

## 1. Real, public data (measured)

| Snapshot | Source | Content | Period |
|---|---|---|---|
| `radiation-may-2024.json` | NASA OSDR RadLab | hourly mean and maximum absorbed dose rate (µGy/h) from DOSTEL1 and DOSTEL2 on the ISS, CRaTER in lunar orbit and MSL-RAD on the surface of Mars | May 2024 |
| `goes-protons-latest.json` | NOAA SWPC, GOES | ≥10 MeV integral proton flux (pfu) and NOAA S-scale level | last day before the build |
| `osdr-studies.json` | NASA OSDR | metadata of spaceflight studies on bone, muscle, heart, radiation, immune system, eyes and sleep | current |
| `donki-sep-may-2024.json` | NASA DONKI | solar energetic particle events | not available: the service was down during every build |

Each snapshot stores its source, URL, retrieval time and SHA-256 in `manifest.json`. The app
checks the hash before using a file.

May 2024 was chosen because it contains a real solar event seen by three detectors: in lunar orbit
CRaTER recorded about 36,858 µGy on 14 May, more than 160 times its typical day; on Mars
Curiosity recorded its largest surge since landing on 20 May; the ISS, shielded by Earth's
magnetic field, shows no comparable spike. Automated tests check these three facts against the
snapshot.

Absorbed dose is converted to an estimated dose equivalent with mean quality factors taken from
published paired measurements: about 2.3 for the ISS (Berger et al., 2017), 4.3 for the lunar
surface (Zhang et al., 2020) and 3.0 for the Mars surface (Hassler et al., 2014). Dose equivalent
approximates, but is not identical to, the effective dose used by NASA limits; the app labels it
as an estimate.

## 2. Simulated data (labelled "Simulated" everywhere)

Real astronaut health records are privacy-protected and cannot be used. The demo crew is three
fictional people (A. Rivera, K. Tanaka, M. Okafor) generated by a seeded random process:

- each person has their own mean and spread for every vital sign, within published adult ranges,
  reflecting the large individual differences seen in Mars-500;
- daily check-ins (sleep, mood, stress, exercise, hydration, symptoms) follow the same rules;
- scenarios apply documented patterns on top: solar storm, short sleep, CO2 excursion, fever,
  skipped exercise and combinations;
- the same seed always produces the same data, so tests and the demo are reproducible.

The anomaly model is trained on simulated healthy days only; see [model-card.md](model-card.md).

## Personal data

None. No real names, dates of birth, identifiers or health records of real people are used,
stored or transmitted.
